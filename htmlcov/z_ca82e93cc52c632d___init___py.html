1"""The model zoo — L0 surface programs that keep the machinery honest. 

2 

3Each entry is a builder returning a ZooModel: an IR Program, its input 

4tensors (weights and activations are both just inputs — parameters are not 

5special at L0), the output var, and a pure-numpy reference the program must 

6match. Entries double as regression tests now and as benchmarks for every 

7later level (peak memory, sharding, fusion, scheduling). 

8 

9Chosen to SPAN mechanisms, not to crawl the architecture gallery: 

10- gpt2: LayerNorm + causal MHA + GELU MLP + residuals (the baseline canon) 

11- llama_block: RMSNorm, RoPE (pairs as born-structured dims — no splits), 

12 GQA (kv heads repeated by declaration), SwiGLU 

13- attention variants: sliding-window mask, output gating, QK-norm, and the 

14 online-softmax (flash) accumulator as a composite reducer whose backward 

15 is DERIVED 

16- physics: 2D heat (fold + Dirichlet ghosts via pad), 1D FDTD on a charted 

17 staggered grid (exact half-integer charts, recharted differences) 

18- moe: capacity-factor top-k routing — the §1.9 indexing family end to 

19 end (argtopk, aligned take, prefix-sum slots, over-scatter, take-back) 

20- trainer: the unrolled trainer (§6.7) — K sampled decode chunks in ONE 

21 Program: §1.8 straight-through top-k sampling over the §1.9 family, 

22 KV reuse as ordinary dataflow (pad+add concat), checkpoint-replay gate 

23 

24Recorded boundaries (LEVELS.md): KV-cache decode (mutation), dynamic 

25shapes, fully dynamic MoE dispatch (a dynamic-shapes problem). 

26"""
