1"""The staging family — declared, never convention (240 C1/C2). 

2 

3Two kinds of compile-time citizens, one doctrine: anything that runs at 

4LOWER TIME and shapes IR is DECLARED with a decorator from this module; 

5recognition is declaration-first at every tier, and the undeclared case 

6refuses loudly. Both are ordinary functions — functional and composable 

7by ruling; sequences of transformations compose from smaller ones. 

8 

9- ``@macro`` — a VALUE-SPACE lowering macro: called inside a body, it 

10 receives the lowering context and the already-lowered argument IR and 

11 emits transformed IR into the same region (``with_respect_to`` is the 

12 archetype). IR-in, IR-out, at the call site. 

13 

14- ``@staged`` — a FUNCTION-SPACE staged transform: it may be host-called 

15 during body lowering and returns a function CITIZEN (an fp-carrying 

16 Handle/Derived) whose IR comes from a registered build rule when it 

17 lowers (``value_and_grad`` is the archetype). Citizen-in, citizen-out; 

18 the "generated function" model, fp-keyed and cache-efficient. When one 

19 executes during lowering, the lowerer records the call as a REPLAYABLE 

20 RECIPE against its result; at launch the recipe chain re-applies the 

21 transforms to the CURRENT parameter values, so a warm hit never serves 

22 a stale capture. Recipes chain through composition: ``t2(t1(f))`` 

23 restages through both. 

24 

25The contract both kinds must honor (device parity, 240 §I.4): output may 

26depend on the inputs' IDENTITY (fp — code and types), never on captured 

27runtime VALUES; values flow only at execution. Structural host 

28evaluation (numbers, tuples, charts) stays implicit — the doctrine 

29governs function citizens only. 

30""" 

31
