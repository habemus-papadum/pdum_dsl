1"""Descent licenses — the SCHEMA STUB (200 §4 interior / §8 runway). 

2 

3A license is a DECLARATION that a lowering may replace the reference 

4denotation with a cheaper equivalent, with the claim stated honestly: 

5what kind of deviation, over what equivalence, within what tolerance, on 

6what input domain. The reference NEVER licenses itself — it is the 

7denotation; licenses are consumed at DESCENT (L4), where the license set 

8joins the registry key (two programs lowered under different license 

9sets are different artifacts). The numeric tier monitors divergence and 

10never certifies it. 

11 

12This module is deliberately data-only: the schema and one worked 

13declaration (the GEMM the spec's worked check names — f16 tiles with f32 

14accumulators). Enforcement, monitoring, and the certified-rewrite 

15checker arrive with L4; nothing here executes. 

16""" 

17
